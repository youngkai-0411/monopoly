# 1. MỤC TIÊU

Visual direction hiện tại về cơ bản đã tốt:

- Low-poly.
- Isometric.
- Pastel.
- Cream / Teal / Gold.
- Vietnamese travel/property board game.
- Mobile Landscape first.

Vấn đề chính KHÔNG phải game thiếu màu.

Vấn đề là:

> Gameplay information chưa có visual hierarchy đủ rõ.

Người chơi cần nhìn Board và nhanh chóng hiểu:

1. Property thuộc Group nào?
2. Property thuộc Player nào?
3. Property đã phát triển tới đâu?
4. Player đang ở đâu?
5. Ai đang chơi?
6. Property nào đang được xem?
7. Destination hiện tại ở đâu?
8. Game đang muốn người chơi chú ý vào đâu?

Mục tiêu:

> **Make the board more informative, not simply more colorful.**

# 4. CURRENT KNOWN PROBLEMS

Current implementation/audit identified:

### Ownership

Ownership currently uses a Player Color strip/mesh.

Problem:

- may visually compete with Group Band;
- orientation/placement is not always ideal;
- can overlap/conflict with Group identity.

### Player Tokens

Current dark/black ground rings have too much visual weight at medium distance while tokens become difficult to identify at Overview distance.

### Player Color Collision

Potential collisions exist between:

- Mint Player vs Grass.
- Yellow Player vs Yellow Group / Gold Focus.
- Blue Player vs Blue Groups / Teal architecture.
- Red Player vs Red Group / danger semantics.

### Selected Property

UI can show one selected Property while the 3D Board does not provide an equivalent world-space selection cue.

### Destination

Destination cue is currently too weak, particularly in Overview.

### Buildings

Player Color is involved too heavily in building ownership representation.

At higher development level the ownership signal can become inconsistent.

### Multiple Players

Players occupying the same tile require a deterministic visual formation rather than overlapping.

together.

Color must not carry every piece of gameplay information.

# 9. FLAG DESIGN

Flag must:

- fit low-poly art direction;
- be simple;
- remain recognizable at Medium camera;
- remain detectable at Overview when possible;
- not cover Property text;
